Support

Frequently asked questions

How do I export a ChatGPT conversation to PDF?
Open the chat, hit Share to get a public URL, paste it in the converter above, choose PDF, and click Generate. The file downloads straight to your device. You can also paste raw conversation text directly.
Which AI chatbots are supported?
ChatGPT share links, Claude conversations, Gemini chats, and raw pasted text from any AI chatbot. The tool auto-detects "Human/Assistant", "You/ChatGPT", and similar speaker patterns.
What export formats are available?
PDF (with Light, Dark, Sepia, or Minimal themes), Markdown (.md), and HTML. All formats preserve code blocks, tables, lists, and headings.
Is this free and unlimited?
Yes — completely free and unlimited. Export as many chats as you like in any format, with no hidden limits.
Is my conversation data stored anywhere?
No. All conversion happens locally in your browser. No data is sent to any server. Nothing is stored, logged, or analyzed — ever.
Does it work on mobile?
Yes. The site is fully mobile-responsive. You can paste a link and download the PDF from any phone browser without installing anything.
What is the table of contents feature?
When enabled, the tool scans your conversation for headings (markdown # H1, ## H2 etc.) and generates a table of contents at the top of the document. Ideal for long research conversations.
Can I preview before downloading?
Yes! Click the Preview button after pasting your content. A full-screen modal shows the formatted document exactly as it will look in the exported file. You can download directly from the preview.
